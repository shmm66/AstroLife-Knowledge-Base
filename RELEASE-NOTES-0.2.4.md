# AstroLife Knowledge Base 0.2.4

- Added 16 bilingual editorial/storytelling cards (8 Persian and 8 English) for annual horoscope writing: two-layer output, transit chronology and six life-area storylines.
- Registered five international practitioner references (CHANI and Cafe Astrology) for sign-by-sign structure, life-area organisation and transit timelines.
- Added a clear report pattern: concise technical evidence (planet, sign, solar house, dates) followed by a short human, forward-looking storyline in the same topic section.
- Updated editorial policy to separate measured astronomy, symbolic interpretation and editorial method; no external forecast prose or images are copied.
- The annual solar-horoscope engine now combines one long-term transit with up to two shorter timing windows and makes the narrative sensitive to the activated solar house.
- Revalidated the full bilingual corpus: 172 cards (86 Persian, 86 English), 45 registered sources, source resolution, language parity, schema fields, licenses and limits.
- Retrieval ranker remains at 228 labelled judgements across 27 bilingual query families; the release adds editorial cards but does not claim that ranker metrics validate astrology.
