# AstroLife Knowledge Base 0.2.0

## Contents
- 136 bilingual cards: 68 Persian and 68 English.
- 25 registered sources, including NASA/JPL planetary physical parameters, Astronomy Engine API provenance, Synergy and Astrology taxonomy, Astrodienst chart types and Cafe Astrology report taxonomy.
- 8 IAU planets and 5 JPL-listed dwarf planets in a structured, unit-labelled physical reference dataset, with explicit uncertainty policy.
- Chart/report taxonomy for natal, transit, progression, solar return, lunar return, synastry, composite, horary and electional requests.
- Separate labels for Western tropical, Vedic/sidereal and Chinese traditions and psychological, traditional and evolutionary interpretive lenses.
- Relationship reports distinguish synastry from composite methods.
- Updated bilingual retrieval ranker: 168 labelled judgements across 17 bilingual query families; grouped holdout 40 judgements; accuracy 0.900, precision 1.000, recall 0.800, F1 0.889; ranking P@3 1.000, Recall@3 0.625, MRR 1.000, nDCG@5 0.996.

## Quality notes
- The holdout is small and derived from seed labels; it is not an independent benchmark. Do not promote as production-grade until a larger, independently labelled Persian/English evaluation is complete.
- Practitioner websites inform category organization only. Original wording is used; copyrighted interpretations are not copied.
- Medical astrology is not used for diagnosis or health advice.
- Astrological interpretations remain symbolic reflection and are not scientifically established predictions.
- A live numerical comparison with JPL Horizons was not completed for this release. The app's coordinate frame and correction conventions are disclosed and must not be described as a validated DE440/DE441 match.
- "Reviewed" is editorial review of this seed corpus, not external peer review.
