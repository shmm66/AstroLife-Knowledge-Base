# Editorial and Evidence Policy

## Four evidence classes
1. `astronomical_measurement`: numerical position/time/coordinate data. Must include provider, version, epoch, time scale, reference frame, observer, options and test limits.
2. `historical_tradition`: what a named historical astrological text or school taught. This establishes the history of an idea, not that the idea predicts real events.
3. `symbolic_reflection`: contemporary, original interpretation written as an invitation to reflect. It is not a measured personality trait.
4. `empirical_research`: published tests or reviews of astrological claims. Include design and limitations; represent significant critiques when they exist.

## Wording standard
- Good: "در تفسیر سنتی، زهره را با محبت و ارزش‌های شخصی مرتبط می‌دانند. می‌توانید از این بخش برای فکرکردن به نیازهای عاطفی خود استفاده کنید."
- Bad: "زهره ثابت می‌کند که شما حتماً در ماه آینده ازدواج می‌کنید."
- Good: "این دو جنبه در چارت با فاصلهٔ زاویه‌ای ثبت شده‌اند؛ معنای نمادین آن‌ها به مکتب تفسیری وابسته است."
- Bad: "این جنبه از نظر علمی سازگاری ۹۲ درصدی شما را ثابت می‌کند."

## Required checks before publishing an entry
- Is the statement an astronomy fact, historical claim, symbolic interpretation or empirical claim?
- Is the source primary/official for the claim being made?
- Is the source allowed to be reused? If unclear, publish a link and an original summary only.
- Are source title, author/publisher, URL, access date and applicable license recorded?
- Does the entry distinguish observation from interpretation?
- Is there a plain-language Persian and English version?
- Does it avoid deterministic, frightening or high-stakes advice?
- Is it meaningfully different from existing cards?
- Has a reviewer approved the candidate?

## Evidence limits
A correct planetary coordinate does not validate an astrological interpretation. Controlled studies have tested some natal-astrology claims, and their methods and interpretations have been debated. The library must describe the research accurately rather than claiming either that a chart is scientifically predictive or that one paper resolves every possible question.
